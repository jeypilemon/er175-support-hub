ER175A FI Support Hub

Privacy Policy

A straightforward explanation of the limited information used by ER175 Hub and the third-party services visitors may choose to access.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. About this website

Motorstar ER175A FI Support Hub (“ER175 Hub”, “we”, or “this website”) is an independent, community-created reference project. It provides motorcycle parts references, owner resources, maintenance information, troubleshooting guides, and CVT setup tools. It is not an official website of Motorstar, Honda, or any motorcycle manufacturer or marketplace.

2. Information stored on your device

The website does not currently require visitor accounts. If shared search analytics are enabled, submitted search terms are sent to the project analytics spreadsheet and aggregated to show popular searches. Search analytics do not require an account and are not intended to identify individual visitors. To make search more convenient, the search feature can save up to six recent search terms in your browser’s local storage. This recent-search information stays in that browser on that device and is not sent to the website by the recent-search feature. You can remove it by clearing this site’s stored browser data.

Your selected light or dark theme may also be saved in local storage so the site can remember your preference.

3. Hosting and technical information

When you visit a website, its hosting and delivery providers may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps for security, reliability, and service operation. Any such processing is governed by the relevant provider’s privacy terms. We do not use the website’s local recent-search feature to build a visitor profile.

4. Catalog data and external services

Parts and reference information may be loaded from published Google Sheets data sources. The website also links to or may display content from third-party services such as YouTube, online marketplaces, and payment providers. When you open an external service, that provider may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those services or their data practices.

5. Marketplace and affiliate links

Some product links may be affiliate links. If you choose to make a purchase through one of them, the site operator may receive a small commission, at no additional cost to you. This helps cover hosting, maintenance, and development. Product references are provided to help visitors research options; always review the seller’s listing, specifications, compatibility, price, and return terms before buying.

6. GCash and payment QR

If you choose to scan the support QR code, the payment or transfer is handled by the payment provider and participating financial networks. Their privacy, security, and transaction policies apply. The website does not receive your payment credentials through the QR image itself.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to use external links or the support QR code. You can clear locally stored search history and theme preferences through your browser’s site-data controls. Browser and hosting-provider records, where applicable, are subject to those providers’ own retention practices.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when website features or services change. For questions about this policy, contact the project through the official social links listed on the ER175 Hub homepage.
