What's New

A quick look at the features and improvements available in the ER175 Hub.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Recent improvements

ER175 Hub is maintained as a practical reference for parts research, owner information, troubleshooting, and CVT setup exploration. This page highlights notable improvements; small fixes and behind-the-scenes maintenance may not be listed individually.

Search and product discovery

  • Live suggestions for matching product names and categories while typing.
  • Recent searches are saved locally in the visitor’s browser for convenience.
  • Search suggestions and recent searches work without creating an account.

Interface and accessibility

  • Light and dark themes with a remembered visitor preference.
  • Responsive navigation, clearer controls, and improved contrast across key sections.
  • Refined product image viewing and modal interactions.

Motorcycle reference tools

  • Parts finder with category filters and marketplace references.
  • Owner resources, maintenance references, and EFI troubleshooting guides.
  • CVT setup comparison and educational performance estimates.
Have a correction or a suggestion for a future improvement? Send it through the project’s official social channels linked on the homepage.
